The suburbs
woke up

Sweden stayed home. The suburbs went out and voted.

The 2026 general election (preliminary, 14 Sep) compared with 2022 (final). Stockholm, Göteborg and Malmö. The image is an illustration (AI).

67to10

That is how many election districts in the three biggest cities had turnout below 50 %. First 2022, then 2026. A drop of 85 percent, while the country as a whole voted less.

Göteborg as a whole fell too

Municipal level. The city lost 2,7 percentage points, while Rannebergen and Eriksbo in the same city rose 6 to 8. The contrast is the story.

Before you quote this

  • The 2026 figures are preliminary.Election-night count. Final turnout tends to land about 3 percentage points higher.
  • Districts have been redrawn.Many election districts got new boundaries ahead of 2026. The comparisons cover districts that Valmyndigheten classes as comparable.
  • The contrast is the story.The national trend points down at the same time as the suburban districts climb sharply. Not one or the other, but both at once.
  • Which areas this is about.Districts with previously low turnout are often suburbs with large immigrant populations and lower average income.
