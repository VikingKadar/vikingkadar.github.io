The Royal
Opera

DoneAug 2026Cross-programme project, Berghs

How do you get 20-year-olds to pick the opera over Netflix?

My role: idea and concept; the group refined and packaged it together.

In short

  1. What I didIdea and concept: Same feeling, different stage. Three posters, two AI films and the AI guide Madame O.
  2. The proofA funnel with a metric at every step: UTM per placement, and Madame O logging every chat as an event, from picked drama to ticket click.
  3. What I learnedA concept is a format, not a picture. And recognition without a next step is wasted.

The opera doesn't have a relevance problem. It has a recognition problem. So we borrowed feelings people already have and put them on a different stage.

Scroll sideways, click to enlarge. AI visualisations of the concept; a live campaign would be produced with real people and real photography.

01

Insight

Young adults already live on drama: series, football, the group chat. They know every feeling the opera is built on. The question isn't "will I like it?" but "will I fit in?"

02

Idea

Same feeling, different stage. One familiar feeling, one real performance, another stage. Football, gossip, heartbreak. A format, so it can run forever.

We don't change the art. We change how it's introduced.

03

Impact

A funnel with a metric at every step: UTM per placement, and Madame O logging every chat as an event, from picked drama to ticket click. So we learn what converts, not just what gets seen.

Media

Geofencing

The football poster shows up on phones around the stadiums on match nights. Gossip and heartbreak run downtown on nights out. We meet people in the middle of the feeling, not after it.

Proof

Heart rate

We track heart rates during what people think are everyday moments: a job interview, a first date, a decisive match. Then the reveal: the spike came from a seat inside the Opera. The concept proves itself. Made in Seedance.

Conversion

Madame O

An AI guide that asks: love, betrayal, jealousy or murder? She matches you with a performance, spills the tea on the plot and answers everything a first-timer doesn't dare to ask.

  • Format beats picture.A strong concept is a format. That's what lets it grow.
  • Recognition needs a next step.Madame O takes you from "that's so me" to a ticket.
  • Placement is the message.The same poster means more outside a stadium at 9 pm than at a bus stop at 10 am.

The road there

Three ideas were pitched. One went forward because it could be repeated and measured.

01

You already know the opera

Pitched

Flash mob: two people from the target group start arguing in the street and slide into opera and ballet. Filmed, spread, and it shatters the image of opera as boring.

02

Same feeling, different stage

Chosen

Posters, tabloid covers and merch take the feeling from the stage out into the city. Became the whole campaign.

03

Human certified

Pitched

Once AI content has to be labelled in the EU, the Opera labels its performances with the opposite: Human made.

What I
learned

Two sentences I kept.

1

A concept is a format

Not a picture. A format is what lets an idea run for years instead of one campaign.

2

Recognition needs a next step

"That's so me" is worth nothing on its own. Madame O is what turns it into a ticket.
