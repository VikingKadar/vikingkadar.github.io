The Royal
Opera

How do you get 20-year-olds to pick the opera over Netflix? Berghs, 2026.

The opera doesn't have a relevance problem. It has a recognition problem. So we borrowed feelings people already have and put them on a different stage.

Scroll sideways, click to enlarge. AI visualisations of the concept; a live campaign would be produced with real people and real photography.

01

Insight

Young adults are already drowning in drama: series, concerts, football, the group chat. They know every feeling the opera is built on. They just don't recognise themselves as opera-goers. The question isn't "will I like it?" but "will I fit in?"

02

Idea

Same feeling, different stage. A format, not a picture: one familiar feeling, one real performance, another stage. Football becomes collective release. Gossip becomes La Traviata. Heartbreak becomes Madama Butterfly. It can be extended forever.

We don't change the art. We change how it's introduced.

03

Impact

The campaign is a funnel with a metric at every step. Posters and films get UTM tags per placement. Madame O logs every conversation as an event: the drama you picked, the show she suggested, the click to tickets. Then we know whether gossip converts better than heartbreak, and whether the stands beat Saturday night downtown.

Media

Geofencing

The football poster shows up on phones around the stadiums on match nights. Gossip and heartbreak run downtown on nights out. We meet people in the middle of the feeling, not after it.

Proof

Heart rate

We track heart rates during what people think are everyday moments: a job interview, a first date, a decisive match. Then the reveal: the spike came from a seat inside the Opera. The concept proves itself. Made in Seedance.

Conversion

Madame O

An AI guide that asks: love, betrayal, jealousy or murder? She matches you with a performance, spills the tea on the plot and answers everything a first-timer doesn't dare to ask.

Outside the box, but with a through-line. That's the balance I chase: as far out as you can go without losing grip of what's being sold.Summary of client feedback. My role: idea and concept; the group refined and packaged it together.
  • A strong concept is a format. That's what lets it grow.
  • Recognition without a next step is wasted. Madame O takes you from "that's so me" to a ticket.
  • Placement is the message. The same poster means more outside a stadium at 9 pm than at a bus stop at 10 am.

The road there

Three ideas were pitched. One went forward because it could be repeated and measured.

01

You already know the opera

Pitched

Flash mob: two people from the target group start arguing in the street and slide into opera and ballet. Filmed, spread, and it shatters the image of opera as boring.

02

Same feeling, different stage

Chosen

Posters, tabloid covers and merch take the feeling from the stage out into the city. Became the whole campaign.

03
